Emily Yue-ting Jia

Contact me at eyjia@usc.edu or jiaemily120@gmail.com

I’m Emily Yue-ting Jia, a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Southern California, co-advised by Prof. Yajie Zhao and Prof. Yue Wang.

My research focuses on 3D computer vision and generative models. I’m also interested in learning physical dynamics from visual data and applying this understanding to robotics.

I received my bachelor’s degree in Computer Science and Technology from Tsinghua University, where I was fortunate to work with Prof. Li Yi and Prof. Yushen Liu on 3D computer vision and scene reconstruction.
