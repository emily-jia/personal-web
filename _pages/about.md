I'm Emily Yue-ting Jia, a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Southern California, co-advised by [Prof. Yajie Zhao](https://www.yajie-zhao.com/) and [Prof. Yue Wang](https://yuewang.xyz/).

My research focuses on 3D computer vision and generative models. I'm also interested in learning physical dynamics from visual data and applying this understanding to robotics.

I received my bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Technology from Tsinghua University, where I was fortunate to work with [Prof. Li Yi](https://ericyi.github.io) and [Prof. Yushen Liu](https://yushen-liu.github.io) on 3D computer vision and scene reconstruction.
